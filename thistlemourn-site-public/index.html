THE GAME

Steel, spirits and a living world.

Thistlemourn is moving toward a Japanese-inspired open-world RPG built around exploration, samurai combat, character progression, equipment, quests and mythology. Only the village, samurai character and default katana are treated as established current work; the systems below are the plan.

THE WORLD

From the first village outward.

The starter village defines the new direction. The surrounding regions are clearly separated into current, planned and future concepts.

CURRENT CHARACTER

The Samurai.

The current player-character direction is a realistic samurai suited to the new Japanese UE5 setting. Future systems will let equipment, build and allegiance shape the character rather than forcing a single rigid class.
